Privacy · Local application

Privacy policy

Tachy is designed to operate locally, without a user account, advertising or profiling.

1. No collection by the application

The Tachy Android application does not collect personal data. It requires no account, creates no user profile and does not send your personal results to a server.

2. Local and offline operation

Tachy’s essential features run locally on your device. No remote database is required for training, recording results or reviewing your progress.

3. Results, scores and settings

Your results, scores, any history and settings are stored locally on your device. Tachy does not transmit them. They can be removed through available app controls or by clearing the application’s data in Android.

4. Advertising and tracking

The application contains no advertising and no individual tracking for advertising purposes. The website may use Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregated traffic statistics, without cookies or individual advertising profiles.

5. Permissions

Tachy limits permission requests to those needed for its operation. Android displays any permissions requested and lets you manage them in your device settings.

6. Third-party services

The application does not rely on third-party services to transmit or store your personal results. The website is hosted with GitHub Pages and may use Cloudflare for domain resolution, security and aggregated analytics.

7. Contact form

When you use the Contact page, the information you enter is sent to Form.taxi to route your message and prevent spam. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information. This data is not used to create an advertising profile.

8. Security

Local operation reduces data transfers, but no device or computer system can guarantee absolute security. Protect access to your device and keep Android and the application up to date.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may change to reflect updates to the application, website or services used. The version published on this page is the current version.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy or Tachy, use the Contact page exclusively.
